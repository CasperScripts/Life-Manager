Personal advice

Apple Health & Siri

This is a Safari / Home Screen web app. It cannot read Apple Health or register native Siri intents.

To get Health + Siri later you’d need a native iOS wrapper (Swift / Capacitor) with HealthKit and App Intents / Shortcuts. Until then, use manual steps/sleep logging in Health, and Shortcuts can’t inject data into this web app securely.

Data & privacy
